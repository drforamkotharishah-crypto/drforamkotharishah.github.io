The Physician

Your neighbourhood doctor, trained for emergencies

Dr. Foram Kothari Shah completed her MBBS at K.J. Somaiya Medical College, Sion, and her Masters in Emergency Medicine (Society for Emergency Medicine, India, 2022) with training at Hinduja Hospital. She also has clinical experience at Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital.

She looks after families in Thakur Village, Thakur Complex and the surrounding neighbourhood — from everyday illnesses to long-term conditions like BP, diabetes and thyroid — and visits elderly patients at home. She has a special interest in joint pain and arthritis care.

Important Information

Online consultations follow the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020) issued under the National Medical Commission and are for patients located in India. All consultations are conducted by Dr. Foram Kothari Shah, registered with the Maharashtra Medical Council. Patient consent is taken before every online consultation.

Digital tools may be used to organise your history and reports for the doctor. All clinical decisions, diagnoses and prescriptions are made only by the doctor. A prescription is issued only when clinically appropriate and is not guaranteed.

As clinic policy, online prescriptions are limited to a maximum of 3 days of medicines, and medicines restricted for telemedicine are not prescribed online.

Online consultation is not a substitute for emergency care. For chest pain, breathlessness, unconsciousness, severe bleeding or any life-threatening situation, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

Your personal and health information is kept confidential and used only for your care, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Health updates are shared with family members only with the patient's consent.
