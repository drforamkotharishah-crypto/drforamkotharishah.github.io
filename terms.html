Services

Dr. Foram Kothari Shah, MBBS, is registered with the Maharashtra Medical Council. The clinic offers clinic consultations, home visits and online video consultations. Fees are listed on the Refund & Cancellation page and on the home page.

Online consultations

  • Online consultations follow the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020, and are for patients located in India.
  • Your consent is taken before every online consultation.
  • All diagnoses and prescriptions are decided only by the doctor. A prescription is issued only when clinically appropriate.
  • Online prescriptions are limited to a maximum of 3 days of medicines. Medicines restricted for telemedicine are not prescribed online.
  • The doctor may ask you to visit the clinic, get tests done or see a specialist if an online consultation is not enough.

Not for emergencies

Online consultation is not a substitute for emergency care. For chest pain, breathlessness, unconsciousness, severe bleeding or any life-threatening situation, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

Your responsibilities

  • Use the prescription only for the patient it was issued for.
  • Be on time. Late arrival may shorten the consultation.
