Last updated: 19 October 2026

Rescheduling

You may reschedule free of charge up to 1 hour before your appointment by WhatsApp or call on +91 98203 51533.

When the fee is refunded in full

  • The clinic cancels your appointment, or the doctor is unavailable. You may choose a full refund or a free new slot.
  • You were charged twice for the same appointment. The extra payment is refunded.
  • Money was debited but the payment failed or was not received by the clinic.
  • The online consultation could not take place because of a technical problem on the clinic's side, and you do not want a new slot.

When the fee is not refunded

  • You cancel less than 1 hour before the appointment.
  • You do not join or attend the appointment (no show).
  • The consultation took place. A prescription is given only when the doctor finds it clinically appropriate, and a fee is not refunded because a prescription or a particular medicine was not given.

How to ask for a refund

Send your name, appointment date and payment ID on WhatsApp to +91 98203 51533 or email drforam21@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method (UPI, card or net banking) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.
